Why we work this way

I spent six years at a firm where a living room cost eleven thousand dollars and four months.

Most of both went on meetings. I would drive across town to look at a wall I had already been sent eleven photographs of, and the client paid for the drive at a designer’s rate. The part that actually changed the room — the plan, the palette, the list of what to buy — took about a fortnight of real work.

So Alcovea sells that fortnight, and nothing around it. You send photographs and a few measurements. A scaled plan, a palette fixed against your own light, a priced list and an install guide come back. Everything a design firm normally bills for in between is the part I removed on purpose, and the price is what is left when you take it out.

People ask whether a room can really be designed by someone who has not stood in it. One hundred and forty-two times now, in seven states, the answer has been yes — provided the measurements are right, which is why we would rather redraw a plan for free than guess.

How the studio works

What a design firm bills for,and what we do instead.

  1. Removed

    The site visit

    Two hours in the room, four in the car, billed to you at a designer’s rate.

    Instead

    4photographs and a tape

    Corners, not the doorway. If a measurement is wrong we redraw it free, and no plan has gone out on a bad number.

  2. Removed

    The hourly rate

    The meter that makes a client hesitate before asking a question.

    Instead

    0re-quotes since 2019

    One price per room, fixed before we start, and the plan is redrawn free until the day the pack ships.

  3. Removed

    The supplier commission

    The quiet fifteen per cent that decides which sofa ends up on your list.

    Instead

    $18,400passed back last year

    You buy direct at the price we found, and the trade discount goes onto your list rather than into ours.

  4. Removed

    The open-ended timeline

    Four months for a living room, most of it spent waiting on someone else’s diary.

    Instead

    5rooms per intake

    Intake opens monthly and closes at five. It is why the average pack lands in 11 days against a promised 14.
