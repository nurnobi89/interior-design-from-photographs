How they sit together

One flat, five rooms,five separate packages.

We price per room because rooms are drawn per room. A flat like this one is five packages, taken in whatever order suits the budget — and each second room in the same order takes 15% off.

Most people start with the room they use after dark, which is usually 01. The room people regret leaving last is 03, because a bedroom is the one you see first thing every morning.

The one we do not

Whole houses, all at once.We say no, and here is why.

A five-room house drawn in one go is four months of work for us and a $12,000 bill for you, which is the model this studio was built to replace.

  • Rooms get built one at a time anyway. Nobody furnishes five rooms in a fortnight, and a plan drawn ten months before the money arrives is a plan drawn against last year’s prices.
  • The list goes stale. Our links stay live for ninety days because that is how long a price can be trusted. Multiply that across a house and half the list is fiction.
  • You can still do the whole house. Take the rooms in the order you will actually spend, with 15% off each second room. Two clients have done all five that way, ten months apart.
