How it works

Fourteen days, markedon a calendar.

  1. Day 0

    You send the room

    Four corner photographs, two measurements and the questionnaire. Read the same day.

  2. Day 3

    We draw and price

    The plan first, the palette against your light, then a list with every line in stock.

  3. Day 9

    First draft, two revisions

    Swap a rug, keep a chair, move the desk. The list rebalances around whatever you change.

  4. Day 14

    The pack ships

    Six documents in one PDF. Links live for ninety days, and the average lands on day eleven.

If we miss the promised date, the balance drops 10%. Four packs have, in one hundred and forty-two.

Before you book

Can a room really be designed without a visit?

One hundred and forty-two times so far, in seven states. A plan needs measurements, corner photographs and an honest questionnaire — not a designer in the doorway.

What if I measure something wrong?

Photograph the tape against the wall and we redraw at no cost, up to the day the pack ships.

Is the price really fixed?

Yes. It is agreed before a line is drawn and has never been re-quoted since 2019.

After the pack

Do I have to buy everything on the list?

No. Every line carries a cheaper alternate that still works in the plan, and the room reads the same from the drawing whichever you choose.

What happens when something sells out?

Email us inside ninety days and a same-price alternative comes back within two working days.
